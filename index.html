Locked-Door Deadline

Welcome to the Locked-Door Deadline story game. Make your choices wisely!

You are an investigative student editor at the school paper. Rumors have been swirling all day about a breach: a rare flash drive containing the upcoming final exam answers has vanished from the locked media lab.

Worse, the administration may be pinning the theft on you to silence your reporting. With the automated security lockdown engaging at 7:00 PM, uncover the culprit and clear your name before morning.
