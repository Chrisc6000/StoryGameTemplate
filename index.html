Locked-Door Deadline

Welcome to the Locked-Door Deadline story game. Make your choices wisely!

You are an investigative student editor at the school paper. Rumors have been swirling all day about a breach: a rare flash drive containing the upcoming final exam answers has vanished from the locked media lab. Worse, whispers in the courtyard claim the administration is pinning the theft directly on you to silence your recent reporting on school policies. With the automated security lockdown engaging at 7:00 PM, you have limited time to navigate the campus, uncover the real culprit, and clear your name before morning.
